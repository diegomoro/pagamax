# Public Beta Monetization Plan

Last updated: 2026-06-06

The public beta should monetize trust and checkout intent, not consumer distraction.

## V1 Positioning

- Consumer app remains free during public beta.
- No consumer ads in v1.
- Sponsored offers are allowed only when clearly labeled and never allowed to override a clearly better user outcome.
- B2B products are merchant and issuer/wallet insights.

## Data Products

Collect and retain data only when disclosed and review-safe:

- Merchant and category
- Amount band, not precise amount when precision is unnecessary
- Recommendation set and ranking position
- Selected method and handoff target
- Return-from-wallet event
- Saved merchant event
- Stale-data exposure
- Sponsored-offer exposure and click
- Approximate city/region only when consented or merchant-derived
- App version and device class
- Outcome feedback entered by the user

Raw sensitive operational events should be short-lived. Long-term commercial value should be stored as pseudonymized cohorts and aggregates.

## Merchant Portal

Initial merchant portal capabilities:

- Merchant login and role-based access
- Offer setup with category, date range, eligibility, budget, and landing/handoff URL
- Sponsored label preview
- Budget pacing and stop controls
- Performance dashboard with exposure, selection, handoff, saved merchant, and feedback aggregates
- Category demand signals and amount-band distribution
- Export of aggregate reports only

## Issuer And Wallet Insights

Issuer/wallet products:

- Promo competitiveness by category and amount band
- Lost and won recommendation share
- Handoff conversion by provider and merchant category
- Stale-data impact and availability gaps
- Sponsored-offer performance when applicable

## Ranking Policy

Primary ranking must optimize expected user outcome. Sponsored offers may appear in clearly labeled placements or as secondary options, but they must not hide or outrank a clearly better organic recommendation.

## Deferred Monetization

Consumer subscriptions, consumer ads, payment splitting, referral commissions tied to payment approval, and full payment orchestration should remain deferred until legal, fraud, data protection, and partner controls are proven.
