# Paga Menos Public Beta Terms

Last updated: 2026-06-06

Paga Menos is an independent recommendation tool for comparing payment methods, wallet apps, bank apps, cards, and discount conditions before the user pays in their own payment app.

## What Paga Menos Does

- Scans or receives a QR/payment link.
- Identifies merchant, amount, category, provider hints, and available discount conditions.
- Ranks payment options by expected user value, speed, and configured preferences.
- Opens the user's selected wallet, bank, or payment app when possible.
- Lets the user save the decision for future reference.

## What Paga Menos Does Not Do

- Paga Menos does not process payments.
- Paga Menos does not approve payments.
- Paga Menos does not move money between users.
- Paga Menos does not store bank credentials, card numbers, bank secrets, or biometric data.
- Paga Menos is not affiliated with banks, wallets, card issuers, merchants, or payment providers unless an affiliation is explicitly stated.

The user always confirms any payment inside their own wallet, bank, or payment app. Issuer, merchant, wallet, and bank terms apply.

## Discounts And Estimates

Discounts, caps, exclusions, dates, stock conditions, category eligibility, card eligibility, wallet eligibility, merchant eligibility, and bank rules can change. Paga Menos estimates expected value from available data, user settings, and cached promotion information. The final amount, discount, refund, or benefit is determined by the issuer, wallet, bank, merchant, or payment provider.

## Sponsored Offers

Sponsored offers may appear in the public beta only when they are clearly labeled as paid or sponsored. Sponsored offers must not override a clearly better user outcome in the primary recommendation ranking.

## Accounts

The public beta may require an account to synchronize preferences, deletion requests, consent settings, diagnostics, and abuse protections. Users are responsible for keeping their email account secure.

## Beta Limitations

The public beta may contain incomplete coverage, stale merchant data, disabled providers, broken wallet links, or estimates that differ from final issuer behavior. Users should verify the final payment details in their payment app before confirming.
